Selected work

  • council-discourse - NYC Council meetings transcribed with all speakers named and meetings chaptered. Inspired by Vikram Oberoi's citymeetings.nyc. Live: council-discourse.pages.dev.
  • crimerisk - national crime risk index at the census block group and tract level for the US, built from public data.
  • lobbying-signals - trend detection over federal lobbying disclosures. Live at umqadir.github.io/lobbying-signals.
  • ecg-clinical - ECG diagnostic models trained at one hospital, evaluated at two others.
  • small-lm-lab - small language models trained from scratch to locate when induction heads form.
  • nettalk - from-scratch NumPy reproduction of NETtalk, an early text to speech system using a neural network w/ 18,629 parameters and a single hidden layer (Sejnowski & Rosenberg, 1987).
