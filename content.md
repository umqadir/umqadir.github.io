## Selected work

- [council-discourse](https://github.com/umqadir/council-discourse) - NYC Council meetings transcribed with named speakers and chaptered, after Vikram Oberoi's citymeetings.nyc. Live at [council-discourse.pages.dev](https://council-discourse.pages.dev).
- [crimerisk](https://github.com/umqadir/crimerisk) - national block-group-level crime-risk surface for the US, built from public data.
- [lobbying-signals](https://github.com/umqadir/lobbying-signals) - trend detection over federal lobbying disclosures. Live at [umqadir.github.io/lobbying-signals](https://umqadir.github.io/lobbying-signals).
- [ecg-clinical](https://github.com/umqadir/ecg-clinical) - ECG diagnostic models trained at one hospital, evaluated at two others.
- [small-lm-lab](https://github.com/umqadir/small-lm-lab) - small language models trained from scratch to locate when induction heads form.
- [nettalk](https://github.com/umqadir/nettalk) - from-scratch NumPy reproduction of NETtalk (Sejnowski & Rosenberg, 1987).
